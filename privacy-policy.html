Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Local Storage & Cookies

This site uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) to save the last nickname and country you entered, so you don't have to retype them on your next run. This data is not used to identify you and is never transmitted elsewhere — it stays only on the browser you're using. Cookies may also be used for ad delivery and usage analytics.

Game Record Database - Firebase

To provide a global leaderboard, this site stores game result data using Google's Firebase (Firestore) service. Only runs that reach the 2048 tile are stored — if a run doesn't reach 2048, nothing about it is saved. For a run that does reach 2048, the elapsed time and an anonymous authentication identifier (a random value that cannot identify you personally) are stored. If you choose to appear on the global leaderboard, the nickname and country you select are also stored and displayed publicly — the country is a value you pick yourself from a list, not detected automatically from your location or IP address. We do not collect personally identifying information such as your name or email, and you can keep playing without ever entering a nickname or country. Stored data is managed in accordance with Firebase's Privacy Policy.

Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

This site may display ads through Google AdSense. Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites. For more information on how Google uses advertising cookies, see Google's Advertising Technology Policy.

Opting Out of Ad Personalization

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

EU/EEA Visitors

For visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), Google's EU User Consent Policy requires user consent before ads are served. This site is currently in the process of applying for Google AdSense approval, and plans to integrate a consent management tool such as Google Funding Choices after approval to comply with applicable regulations.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
