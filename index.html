Rules, for the record

You already know how this works: arrow keys or swipe to slide, same numbers merge, a new tile drops after every move. That part isn't the game. The game is the clock. Every run is timed from the moment you hit Start, and the only number that matters at the end is how fast you built the 2048 tile. No move limit, no undo, no second chances — one clean run, ranked against everyone else who's tried.

Shaving seconds off your run

Anchor your highest tile in a corner and never make a move that pulls it out. Commit to one build direction — always left, always down — instead of reacting tile by tile. Plan two or three merges ahead so you're never forced into a bad move under time pressure, and never let a free merge sit on the board while you chase something else. Every hesitation is time on the clock — play decisively.

How ranking works

Only runs that actually reach the 2048 tile are recorded and ranked — anything else doesn't appear anywhere. If your run falls short, it's not saved at all; the only number that lives on the leaderboard is elapsed time for finished runs, fastest first. Reach 2048 and you can submit a nickname and country to appear on the ranking panel beside the board.

FAQ

Is this game free? Yes — no installation or payment required, just play instantly in your browser.

Are my records saved? Only runs that reach 2048 are saved — your elapsed time is recorded and used to calculate your rank among all 2048 finishers worldwide. Runs that don't reach 2048 aren't stored anywhere. We don't collect personally identifying information — only the nickname and country you choose to enter (optional) are shown publicly on the leaderboard.

What is this based on? This game is an independent fan implementation inspired by the original 2048 by Gabriele Cirulli (released under a CC BY-SA-style open license).
